Why 16 GB Won the Value Tier

Two market facts define the 2026 mid build. First, GDDR7 shortages pushed every new GPU 50–150% over MSRP, but used 16 GB Blackwell cards stayed sane (~$370–420). Second, the RAMpocalypse made system RAM a budget line item — 32 GB DDR5-6000 costs ~$420. The result: a 16 GB tower at ~$1,465, with a clear upgrade path to the full-Q4 experience when you're ready to pay for it.

⚠️ The honest 2026 gap: Gemma 3 27B and GLM-4.7-Flash at full Q4 need 24 GB, and 24 GB (used 4090) now costs ~$2,200–2,400 — a 24 GB build runs ~$3,000–3,200. If full Q4 is the goal, that's the real price of admission this year. See the $3,000 machine.
