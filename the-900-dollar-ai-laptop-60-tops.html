Why the Zenbook 14 Wins the Budget Slot

Three things line up in 2026: the newest small models are genuinely good (Ministral 3, Qwen3.5, Gemma 4 sub-12B handle real work), the RAMpocalypse made big-RAM laptops brutally expensive, and AMD's 60-TOPS XDNA2 NPU became the Copilot+ entry ticket. The Zenbook 14 is where those three meet — a full AI laptop under $1,000 that doesn't sacrifice the OLED display to get there.

💡 The NPU truth: the 60 TOPS number buys battery-efficient always-on AI — voice transcription, background agents, Copilot+ features — not bigger models. RAM decides which models fit; bandwidth decides speed. Don't buy an NPU number expecting a 27B model to run.
