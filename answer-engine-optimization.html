How to Make Your Website Get Cited by AI Assistants (2026)

Published: August 8, 2026 — Search is being replaced by answers. When someone asks ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Google's AI Overviews a question, your site either gets cited in the answer — or it doesn't exist. Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) is the practice of being the source AI engines choose. This guide explains how AI engines pick sources and the exact changes that get your content quoted.

⚡ Quick Takeaways

  • AEO = being quoted, not ranked. AI engines read whole pages and cite direct answers
  • The #1 change: answer the question directly in the first 50–100 words under a matching heading
  • Structured content wins: headings, lists, tables, and JSON-LD schema are citation magnets
  • Authority compounds: corroborating sources, freshness, and crawlability all feed the citation decision

How AI Engines Choose Sources

Classic SEO optimized for a ranking algorithm's keywords. AI engines work differently: they retrieve candidate pages, read them in full, and extract the passage that answers the question — then cite its source. The practical selection factors, in rough priority:

Factor What it means
Direct answer Does the page answer the exact question, plainly, near the top?
Structure Headings, lists, tables, and FAQ blocks make extraction reliable
Corroboration Do other cited sources agree? Engines prefer convergent answers
Freshness Recent, updated content wins for time-sensitive queries
Authority Same signals as SEO — links, brand, expertise — still matter
Crawlability Clean HTML, working sitemap, no paywall/JS walls on the answer

The Five Changes That Actually Work

  1. Front-load the answer. First 50–100 words, under an H2 that mirrors the question. If someone asks "what is GGUF?", the first paragraph must define GGUF — not tease it. (This blog's GGUF post is written exactly this way.)
  2. Use question-matching headings. H2s should be the questions people ask. AI engines chunk by heading; a question heading tells them exactly what the passage answers.
  3. Add a scannable answer block. A "Quick Takeaways" or key-facts box near the top gives extractors a clean citation target — see every post on this site.
  4. Structured data. Article, FAQPage, HowTo, Person, Product JSON-LD — the machine-readable answer key. The JSON-LD cheat sheet is the reference.
  5. Corroborate and update. Cite your sources, date everything, and refresh regularly. AI engines trust convergence and freshness.

FAQ Content Is AEO Gold

A well-structured FAQ is the most extractable format that exists: question headings + direct answers, already in the shape AI engines want. Every FAQ section on this site (including this post's) is a citation target. The rule: write the answer as if it will be quoted verbatim — because it will be.

💡 The mental shift: you're no longer writing for a click; you're writing for a quote. A cited answer reaches everyone who asks that question, even if none of them click through. That's the trade — visibility for attribution — and in 2026 it's the right trade.

Measuring AEO (There's No Dashboard Yet)

  • Manual AI audits: monthly, ask ChatGPT/Perplexity/AI Overviews your key questions; record whether your site is cited and how.
  • Referral traffic: analytics now shows AI-engine referrers (chatgpt.com, perplexity.ai, etc.) — watch the trend.
  • Branded questions: "what is [your product]?" queries in traditional search rise when you're consistently cited.
  • Citation share vs competitors: the honest metric — of 20 AI answers on your topic, how many cite you?

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)?

AEO is optimizing content so AI answer engines — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Copilot — select and cite your site when answering queries. It differs from classic SEO because AI engines read whole pages and prefer direct, structured, verifiable answers over keyword-matching pages.

How do AI engines choose which sources to cite?

Roughly the same factors that make a page credible to a human reader, evaluated at document scale: clear direct answers to the question, structured content (headings, lists, tables), corroborating sources, freshness, authority signals, and technical crawlability. They also heavily favor pages that answer the exact question in the first section.

What is the single most effective AEO change?

Answer the question directly and completely in the first 50–100 words, with a heading that matches how people ask it — then support it with structured content and data. AI engines extract this as the citation. This one change outperforms every other tactic.

Does structured data (JSON-LD) help with AI citations?

Yes — Schema.org structured data (Article, FAQPage, HowTo, Person, Product) is the strongest signal that a page is a machine-readable answer source. It's also what Google and others index for rich results. The JSON-LD cheat sheet in this blog is the practical reference.

Is AEO different from SEO?

Yes, in emphasis: SEO optimizes for rankings and clicks; AEO optimizes for being quoted in an answer. AEO content is more direct, more structured, more citable, and less gated behind "read more" — because the reader never comes to your page; the assistant brings your answer to them.

How do I measure AEO success?

Track citation mentions: search your brand and key topics in ChatGPT, Perplexity, and AI Overviews; check referral traffic from AI engines in analytics (they now send traffic); and monitor branded-question queries. There's no official metric yet — so build the habit of monthly AI-search audits.
